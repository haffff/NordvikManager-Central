# Players and your settings

## The Players window

**View → Players** lists everyone in the game, with their avatar and colour. A green dot marks who's connected right now; connected players are listed first.

{/* SCREENSHOT: img/user-guide/players.png — the Players window with one player online */}

### Managing players (GM)

:::note[GM only]
**View → Manage Players** is the same list with a **Settings** and a **Kick** button on each player.
:::

- **Settings** opens that player's name, avatar and colour, so you can tidy them up.
- **Kick** removes the player from the game. They can join again with the invite link; to keep someone out for good, ban them in the lobby (see [Inviting players](./getting-started-gm/inviting-players.md#managing-players)).

What each player may see and do is set with [permissions](./permissions.md) on maps, cards and tokens.

## Your player settings

**Settings → Player** has two tabs.

**Player** — how others see you in this game:
- **Name**, **Avatar** (a picture from the materials) and **Color** (your name and messages in chat, your markers on the map).

**This browser** — settings kept only in the browser you're using now:
- **Stylesheets:** the game's stylesheets everyone gets (set by the GM), and **My stylesheets**, ones only you see, e.g. larger text. See [Custom styling](./custom-styling.md).
- **Sound volume:** separate sliders for **Music** (the GM's playlists), **Sound effects** (the soundboard) and **Notifications** (new chat message and your-turn sounds).
- **Offline cache:** how much space the game may use to keep downloaded maps, pictures and sounds, so they don't download again next time. **Clear cache** empties it.

## Keyboard shortcuts

**Settings → Keyboard Shortcuts** lists every shortcut, grouped by where it works (the battle map, the game…).

- **Change a key:** click the shortcut and press the new key, then **Save**. Shortcuts are saved to your account, so they follow you to other games and browsers.
- **Add Custom Binding:** bind a key to any command, including ones from addons, with its arguments.
- **Window Management → Dock windows while holding:** the key you hold while dragging a window to dock it (**Shift** by default), or **None** to always dock. Saved in this browser.
- **Battle Map Grid Snapping:** when moving and resizing snaps to the grid. Saved in this browser. See [Map settings](./battle-map/map-settings.md#snapping).
