# Music and sounds

:::note[GM only]
The GM plays music and sound effects for everyone. Players just hear them, and set their own volume in **Settings → Player → This browser** (see [Players and your settings](./players-and-settings.md)).
:::

Sounds are audio files from the game's [materials](./materials.md). Upload them there first, or link a music folder from your computer.

## Playlists

**View → Playlists** is for background music and ambience.

![The Playlists window](/img/user-guide/playlists.png)

- **New Playlist**, then set:
  - **Name** and **Description**.
  - **Playback Mode:** **Sequential** plays the tracks one after another; **Concurrent** plays them all at once, e.g. rain + fire + tavern chatter layered into one ambience.
  - **Shuffle** (Sequential only) and **Repeat**.
  - **Tracks:** pick audio files from the materials.
- **Play / Resume**, **Pause** and **Stop** control it for everyone. Several playlists can play at the same time.
- Each playlist has its own **volume** slider.
- Players who join while music is playing hear it too.

## Soundboard

**View → Soundboard** is for sound effects: a door creaking, a dragon roaring, rain.

- **New Soundboard** groups sounds, with a **Name**, **Description** and its **Sounds**.
- Click a sound to play it for everyone. While it plays its tile lights up with a progress bar; click it again to stop it.
- The loop button in a tile's corner makes that sound repeat until you stop it (rain, a crackling fire). It is saved on the soundboard and applies from the sound's next play.
- **Stop all** silences every sound playing.
- Each soundboard has its own volume.

## How loud things are

A sound's loudness is set in three places, multiplied together:
1. **The file's own volume**, for a quiet or loud recording: **Materials → Edit → Volume (%)**.
2. **The playlist's or soundboard's volume** slider.
3. **Each player's own sliders** for Music and Sound effects, in their browser.

Actions can play and stop sounds and playlists too, e.g. a trap that plays a sound when triggered (see the Addon guide).
