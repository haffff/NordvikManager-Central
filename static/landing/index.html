What's in the spellbook

Everything a session needs

01

A workspace you arrange

Every tool is a panel you can dock, float and tab. Save layouts and share them with your players, so everyone starts with the right screen.

02

Battle maps and layers

Several maps per game, a grid you resize on the map itself, measuring tools and custom layers above or below the tokens, including GM-only and hidden ones.

03

Tokens and turn order

Tokens with bars and status icons, visible to everyone or just the GM. Each map keeps its own initiative tracker with rounds, and players can end their own turn.

04

Cards from addons

Character sheets, items, notes and handouts are cards made from addon templates, with permissions per player. Install a game system and start playing.

05

Chat and dice

Roll from the chat or with a click, whisper to one player or only to the GM, and add your own chat commands.

06

Music and sound

Playlists and soundboards play to everyone in sync, with fades. You set the volume per track; each player sets their own for music, effects and notifications.

07

Actions, no code needed

Automate the game with hooks and steps: change the music when the turn changes, move tokens, update cards. Sandboxed scripts are there when blocks aren't enough.

How it works

Three steps to the table

  1. 1

    The GM runs the server

    Download NordvikManager and start it on your computer. Your game and its files stay with you.

  2. 2

    Create a game, invite players

    Set up maps and cards, install addons for your system, and send your players an invite.

  3. 3

    Players open a link

    They join from the browser at nordvikmanager.pl/client. Nothing to install.
